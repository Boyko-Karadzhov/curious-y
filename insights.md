# Angles

Real-world phenomenon — explain something observable or practical
Historical discovery — why an idea was needed / how thinking changed
Boundary / failure case — where a model stops working and why
Deep connection — reveal an unexpected connection between concepts

# Reasoning ladder
Direct inference — apply one mastered concept to obtain a consequence.
Composition — combine several mastered concepts into a reasoning chain.
Discrimination — distinguish between plausible competing explanations.
Transfer — recognize and apply concepts in an unfamiliar context.
Counterfactual — change/remove an assumption and reason through the consequences.
Synthesis — integrate multiple concepts to explain or resolve a complex phenomenon.
Derivation — reconstruct a result from deeper principles with minimal assumptions.
