# Curious-Y game principles

## Core inspiration

The game models its engagement mechanics from trashy mobile games. Microtransactions would be replaced with requirement for mental strain. We want to reward people for learning and attach the same dopamine addiction loop to learning.

## Battle

Battle shows the progress of the user. It must not require mental strain at all. Reserve all energy for the learning.

Curious-Y is a classic tug-of-war game where two sides spawn units. To eliminate any requirements for mental strain - the whole battle must be automatic.

## Castle

The Castle is where progression is applied. Players build buildings, purchase upgrades.

## Gambling

We try to avoid giving the player specific promises like "spend this and you get that". Instead it is "spend this for a chance to get that or more!". Keep them anticipating. The assumption is that this will trigger the addictive behavior to "try just one more time".

## Learning

We try to give learning tasks that require minimal mental strain - thus main tool for us is multiple choice questions. We don't teach - if the player does not know then they can guess and learn from mistakes.

Players should progress in the game through learning.

A more strainful learning excersise can be introduced only if we build up the reward enough (like providing a premium currency to speed up an upgrade).

We don't need to provide a complete learning course - our assumption having players consistently absorb various facts can help trigger curiosity for them to learn more by themselves. The most important questions for us are "Why" questions. These kinds of questions provide key insights into concepts that assist learning.

## Daily routing

The game should be built around a daily routine that gives the player a reason to play every day. Different days can reward different behaviors to provide extra diversity to the gameplay.

## Everything else

The experience can be expanded in any way that contributes more to the dopamine addiction for the learning process.
