About this prototype

Each weapon is a separate generated sprite, attached to the hand in every frame. The foreground arm is drawn over the equipment. Artifacts will provide stats without appearing on battlefield units. Completed frame combinations are cached before drawing.

This tests equipment attachment and small-scale readability. The generated walk cycle has limited leg variation; production movement needs a stronger contact/pass cycle. The experimental armor overlay does not fit well enough and is off by default; it needs artwork matched to each pose. This study is not yet integrated into combat or the Forge economy.
