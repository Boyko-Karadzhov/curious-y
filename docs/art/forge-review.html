About this prototype

Armor is painted to fit the body in every animation frame, including the collar, waist and arm openings. Weapons stay separate and attach to the hand, so either sword works with either armor set. Artifacts will provide stats without appearing on battlefield units.

This tests equipment fit and readability. The generated walk cycle still needs stronger leg variation. This study is not yet integrated into combat or the Forge economy.
